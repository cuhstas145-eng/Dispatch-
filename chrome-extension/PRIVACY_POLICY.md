# CargoSync Route Planner Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

## Overview

CargoSync Route Planner is a Chrome extension that helps users plan multi-stop routes, estimate road mileage and driving time, view routes on a map, and calculate revenue per mile (RPM).

## Information processed

When a user searches for a location or calculates a route, the extension sends the location text and/or geographic coordinates needed for the requested operation to the following services:

- **Photon** (photon.komoot.io): processes location search queries and returns matching places and coordinates.
- **OSRM** (router.project-osrm.org): processes route coordinates to calculate a road route, distance, and estimated duration.
- **OpenStreetMap tile servers** (tile.openstreetmap.org): provide map images displayed in the extension.

The extension may therefore transmit user-entered location information to these services when the user uses the relevant features. Please review the respective service providers' privacy practices for information about their handling of requests.

## Information stored by the extension

The extension does not require an account and does not operate its own server to collect users' route searches. It does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, passwords, payment card details, browsing history, or website contents. Route inputs and calculations are used to provide the features requested by the user.

## How information is used

Location queries and route coordinates are used to find places, calculate routes, and display maps. They are not used by the extension for advertising, credit decisions, or selling personal information.

## Data sharing

The extension sends location search queries to Photon, route coordinates to OSRM, and map tile requests to OpenStreetMap tile servers as necessary to provide the requested functionality. It does not sell user data. It does not share user data with unrelated third parties for advertising.

## Security and retention

The extension does not maintain its own remote database of users' searches. Requests are handled by the third-party services listed above and may be subject to their own logging, retention, and privacy policies.

## Children's privacy

The extension is a general-purpose route-planning tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.
