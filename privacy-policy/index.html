Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Reliable Freight Transportation LLC collects, uses, and shares information in connection with its transportation and logistics services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, and other information necessary to provide our transportation and logistics services.

2. How We Use Information

We use personal information to coordinate freight shipments, communicate with drivers and brokers, provide delivery updates, and manage business operations.

3. SMS Messaging

By opting in to receive SMS messages, you agree to receive business-related text messages from Reliable Freight Transportation LLC regarding load assignments, pickup and delivery schedules, shipment updates, and transportation services.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

Mobile information and SMS consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may share necessary information with service providers solely to support our business operations.
